About LifeLink

LifeLink is an automated community blood donation platform engineered to bridge the critical gap between blood donors, recipients, hospitals, and blood banks during medical emergencies.

Our Core Objectives:

  • Reduce emergency blood search time from hours to minutes.
  • Provide smart location-based donor proximity matching.
  • Enable instant multi-channel emergency broadcast alerts.
  • Equip donors with digital QR ID cards for rapid hospital verification.
